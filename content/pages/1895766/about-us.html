Welcome to ReelFlies® Fishing Flies and ReelFlies.ca, a division and brand of Digital Barn Ltd., established in 2003: Canada's largest and most popular online fishing fly store. What sets ReelFlies apart is simple: the quality of our flies, the variety of patterns, high-grade materials, branded Japanese hooks and a focus on locally tied patterns. We offer some of the best-value premium flies in North America, and we never compromise on quality.

Who we are

ReelFlies is a 100% Canadian, family-run online fly shop, long established, rapidly growing and award-winning. We are led by fly fishers and headed by Steve B (President), who has over 40 years of fly fishing experience.

We offer nearly 2,000 hand-tied patterns, and add new patterns and variations all the time. Every fly is shown in detailed professional photography with a full description, so you can inspect every detail before you buy.

Who uses our flies

Besides thousands of fellow fly fishers, we supply professional and semi-pro fishing guides across Canada and the USA, fly shops, outfitters and lodges. We have supplied flies to some of the best-known names in fly fishing, including Bill Spicer and the late Colin McKeown of the TV show “The New Fly Fisher”. If you are fishing in Ontario, we recommend two of the guides we supply: Mike Verhoef, head guide and owner of Fly Fitters, and the guides at Grindstone Angling, one of Canada's longest-established outfitters.

Our flies

Many of our flies are tied locally, right here in Canada, all on top-quality hooks from Mustad, Daiichi, Maruto, Dai-Riki, Kamasan and VMC. They are premium quality, equal to or often better than the flies at your local tackle shop. In fact, we wholesale flies to many well-known tackle shops in Canada, so we have likely tied some of the flies already in your box.

Tired of flies that fall apart after one good session? Ours will stand the test of time. We stand behind every fly we sell.

Visit our fly shop

Our fly shop and offices, close to 4,000 sq ft, are on the main street in the heart of Tillsonburg, Ontario, with free parking front and rear. Walk-ins are by appointment: call us toll free at 1 866 250 4071. From here we ship our flies across Canada and the USA.

Help choosing flies

Not sure which flies to pick? Our team, many with over 20 years of fly fishing experience, is happy to help. Call us or use our contact form, and see our Help page and hook size chart.

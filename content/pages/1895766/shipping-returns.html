Shipping in Canada

Free standard shipping in Canada on fly orders over $60. Fly boxes and gear not included.

Every order is picked and packed by hand at our fly shop in Tillsonburg, Ontario. The delivery times below start once your order leaves us, so please allow a little longer during sales and the peak of the fishing season.

Standard Canada Post

Generally 2–14 business days, delivered to your mailbox, with no tracking. Free on fly orders over $60; below that, $X.XX. Fly boxes don't ship this way, and free shipping doesn't cover larger items such as reels or large popper flies.

Expedited Parcel

Generally 1–7 business days: typically 1–2 days within much of Southern Ontario and 5–7 days to Western Canada. Fully tracked and insured. From $Y.YY.

Express

Our fastest option: generally 1–4 business days, with next-day delivery to many major Canadian regions. Fully tracked and insured. From $Z.ZZ.

Your exact rate is shown at checkout, before you pay.

Fishing trip coming up? Add your trip date to the order notes, and choose Expedited or Express for anything time-sensitive. We always do our best to get orders out quickly.

A few things to know

  • PO boxes: couriers such as Purolator, GLS, UPS, Canpar and FedEx need a street address.
  • Remote and rural addresses: extra charges or delivery time may apply where couriers don't normally deliver.
  • Wrong address: a package returned to us because the address was wrong may carry reshipping charges and a 10% restocking fee.
  • Missing package: please contact us if your package hasn't arrived within 28 working days. A delayed or undelivered package must be reported within 60 days of the order date.

Cancellations, Claims and Returns

We stand behind every fly we sell. Here is how changes and problems with an order are put right.

Cancellation: changing your mind before your order ships

If you change your mind, contact us before your order ships. We'll cancel the items you no longer want and refund them. Once an order has shipped, we can't take items back because of a change of mind.

Claim: something wrong with your order

If flies or gear arrive damaged, are missing from your order, or aren't the size, pattern or colour you ordered, contact us with your order number. We'll put it right with a replacement or a refund.

Return: sending items back

We ask for items back only when we need to see them to settle a Claim, and we'll tell you how to send them. Please contact us before you send anything back.

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. The full terms are on Shipping & Returns and in our Privacy Policy.

Sizes

How do hook sizes work?

A bigger hook number means a smaller hook: a size 18 is much smaller than a size 8. Past size 1 the sizes grow again and are written with a slash, from 1/0 up to 6/0, the largest on our chart. Our hook size chart shows every size from 6/0 to 24, with links to the flies tied in each one.

Ordering

Does a fly selection come with a fly box?

No. Our fly selections are priced for the flies alone: the fly box in the photo isn't included. Fly boxes are sold separately.

Can I use a coupon code during a sale?

A few times a year we run a sale on the site. During a sale, no other discount codes or coupons can be added, and sale prices are final. If an item is briefly out of stock during a sale, we include a close alternative.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, until it ships: contact us and we'll cancel the items you no longer want and refund them. Once an order has shipped, we can't take items back because of a change of mind. See Cancellations, Claims and Returns.

Something's wrong with my order. What do I do?

If flies or gear arrive damaged, are missing, or aren't the size, pattern or colour you ordered, contact us with your order number. We'll put it right with a replacement or a refund.

Do you sell to fly shops, guides and lodges?

Yes. Fly shops, guides, outfitters and lodges are welcome to call us about wholesale orders at 1 866 250 4071.

Shipping

Is shipping free?

Free standard shipping in Canada on fly orders over $60. Fly boxes and gear not included. Every other rate is shown at checkout, before you pay.

How long does delivery take?

Standard Canada Post generally takes 2–14 business days, with no tracking. Expedited Parcel generally takes 1–7 business days and Express 1–4, both fully tracked and insured. Allow a little longer for packing during sales and the peak of the fishing season. The details are on Shipping & Returns.

I have a fishing trip coming up. How do I make sure my flies arrive in time?

Add your trip date to the order notes, and choose Expedited or Express at checkout.

I'm outside Canada. Can I order?

In the USA, please order from ReelFlies.com, our US store, with prices in US dollars and tracked USPS shipping at a flat $9.95. From ReelFlies.ca we ship to Canada, the USA and Australia, but not to freight-forwarding companies.

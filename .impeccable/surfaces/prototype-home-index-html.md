# Home page

## Scope

The ReelFlies home page (reelflies.ca first; the same page serves reelflies.com through its variation). Visitor mode: **Persuade**. Built code-led as a standalone prototype in `prototype/home/`, structured so each region maps to a Stencil component or Page Builder region; after approval the owner starts a fresh theme build from it.

## Audience, job, action

- Anglers on a phone, restocking or newly starting: find the right flies by what they are or what they catch, and add several sizes of a pattern fast. Trade buyers use the same page.
- The action, in working form on the first screen: + Add on real best sellers (size strip, stepper), plus search.
- Proof on hand: real product photos and prices, catalogue counts, the Tillsonburg shop, the four confirmed claims in PRODUCT.md. River and lifestyle photography is commissionable from the in-house photographer; every banner can be reshot.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is a riverside stone with the shop's real flies laid out on it, picked up and added on the spot, a fly at a time. It refuses the category's river-lifestyle hero over a row of tiles, and the precious product-on-white grid.

OWN-WORLD: Wet slate carries the hero, the house-pattern band and the footer as photographed stone; dry stone is the shop's working ground; product photos sit as shot on white specimen cards. Beadhead copper is held for buying only (+ Add, Add to cart, the cart count, the order total). Archivo is the one family: expanded heavy for display, condensed for hook-size numerals, normal for text. Light marks state: shade at rest, raking light on the fly in hand, a copper edge on flies already added.

STORY: A first-time angler sees real flies priced per fly, understands nearly 2,000 patterns are reachable by what a fly is or what it catches, and adds sizes without leaving the page. A regular restocks best sellers straight off the stone. Below the fold: what's hatching this month, how hook sizes run, the house patterns, the selections, and the family shop in Tillsonburg that stands behind every fly.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: pale-stone header (logo, Shop flies, wide search, Account, Cart). A full-bleed wet-slate photograph below, river soft along its top edge. Left five columns: the H1 at about 64px, one line on price and promise, then "What it is" and "What it catches" as named pill lists. Right seven columns: six best sellers laid on the stone as white specimen cards (photo, name, price per fly, + Add opening the stepped size strip). The order line is pinned at the foot (added-fly thumbnails, flies, patterns, total, distance to free shipping). Phone: search in the header, the H1, ways in as swipe rows, cards two-up on the stone, the order line pinned.

FORM: Streamside Stone, revised for commissioned photography: position 6 on the first grounded list, locked by the owner over the re-roll's Hatch Chart (position 3), which joins below the fold as an information band. seed:72da4e2941f02151. Signature interaction: pointing at a Fly Type or Species name surfaces the flies it covers while the rest recede into shade; + Add lifts the card into raking light, opens the size strip (largest hook first), and each added fly drops into the order line. Motion: one settle (shade to light, about 220ms ease-out; the drop, about 400ms); reduced motion makes every state instant.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment

Tapping + Add on a fly lying on the stone, picking size 14, and watching that fly drop into the box on the order line.

## Unresolved

- The buy colour: copper by default; brook-trout red and brass are review alternates (owner's call, he found fluoro orange too stark).
- The commissioned shoot: the six slate banners stand in until the photographer delivers the riverbank stone, the Fly Type set and the Species river scenes (shot list delivered with the prototype).
- Hatch chart timing is general Ontario trout-stream data until the owner checks it.
- The pattern count ("nearly 2,000"), lowest price ($0.75) and free-shipping threshold ($60, fly orders) stay owner defaults.

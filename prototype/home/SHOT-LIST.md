# Shot list: Streamside Stone

For the in-house photographer. The home page is built on one idea: a riverside stone with flies laid out on it. Every photo below shares one stone, one light and one season, so the pages read as one place.

## The rules for every frame

- **One stone.** Pick a flat, dark, fine-grained slate or shale slab at the water's edge. Wet it before each frame, because wet stone goes dark and the flies stand out. Use the same slab, or slabs from the same bank, for every frame.
- **One light.** Soft, low, raking side light, early or late in the day, from the same side in every frame. Avoid hard noon sun and flash.
- **The river stays soft.** Where water shows, it sits at the top edge, out of focus (f/2.8 to f/4). It's a hint of place, never the subject.
- **Real flies, as tied.** No props, no hands unless the frame says so, and no rods or reels.
- **Leave room.** The page sets type and cards over the left half of the hero and the bottom third of each plate, so keep those areas as plain stone.
- **Deliver** the full-resolution originals plus a 2400px-wide JPEG of each, sRGB, with no watermark or logo. The product photos keep their own logo; these don't need one.
